1. UK Home & Bedding Brand: Confidential Case Study

Context

A UK-based home and bedding brand selling high-ticket products on Amazon. Employer and account details are withheld.

Challenge

Grow monthly sales while keeping ad spend efficient and the catalog healthy.

My responsibilities

PPC strategy, organic ranking, listings, pricing and promotions, inventory coordination, P&L reporting and weekly action plans for executives.

Floating bars show approximate monthly ranges. The three phases ran in sequence. Combined improvements contributed; no single tactic is claimed as the sole cause.

~£10–12KApprox. monthly PPC spend
~10–12xApprox. advertising ROAS

Strategy

Rebuilt keyword clustering and campaign structure to harvest converting search terms and cut wasted spend; aligned search-centric PPC with organic ranking goals; improved images, storefront and A+ Content; structured pricing and coupons to lift conversion while protecting margin; coordinated 3PL and FBA replenishment; piloted AI-assisted backend indexing and content tools.

Tools

Amazon Brand Analytics, Search Query Performance, Seller Central, Helium 10, Google Sheets / Excel.

Business impact and takeaway

ROAS measures ad efficiency only, not overall profitability. The takeaway: combine ad discipline with listing quality and healthy inventory rather than relying on any single lever.

3. Premium US Home Textiles Brand (HUK Linens LLC)

Context

End-to-end launch of a premium home textiles brand on Amazon US in a highly saturated category.

Challenge

Build sales velocity without letting advertising cost erode margin, and avoid seasonal stockouts.

My responsibilities

Sourcing, listing optimization, cross-channel promotion, PPC, and Q4 inventory planning across the full product lifecycle.

Verified results

Approximately $600K annualized revenue (annualized, not monthly). Aggregate ACOS held below 30%. Q4 safety-stock guidelines eliminated the brand's historical seasonal stockouts.

Takeaway

Ad efficiency and inventory planning protect each other: stockouts waste ranking and ad spend.

4. Amazon US Product Research & Launch (RRAH LLC)

Context

Full-funnel brand launch on Amazon US, July to November 2024.

Challenge

Select products with real demand and turn new listings into organic rank.

My responsibilities

Data-validated product research and Alibaba sourcing of 30+ high-velocity products; search-indexed, conversion-focused listings; PPC campaigns that harvested keywords into organic gains; ongoing Search Query Performance and account health analysis.

Verified results

30+ products researched and sourced. No sales figures are published here because none are claimed.

Takeaway

Launches work best when product validation, listing indexing and PPC are planned together.
